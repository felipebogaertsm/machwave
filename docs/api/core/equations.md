# core.equations

Differential equations solved during internal ballistics simulation.

- **SRM mass balance** — Seidel’s chamber pressure ODE coupling burn rate, burn area, free volume, and nozzle mass flow for solid rocket motors.
- **LRE mass balance** — Chamber pressure ODE for liquid engines driven by injector mass flow rates (oxidizer + fuel) and nozzle discharge.
- **Point-mass trajectory** — Basic 1-DOF ballistic trajectory equations (altitude and velocity derivatives from thrust, drag, gravity).
